How it works

Simple. Intentional.

01

Follow people, not restaurants

Your feed shows places from people you follow. Chronological. No algorithm, no ads, no noise.

02

Share in seconds

Photo, place name, a quick take. That's it. No long reviews. If it's worth sharing, it should take less than 20 seconds.

03

Save places you want to try

Bookmark any place in one tap. Come back when you're hungry. Your saved places are always there.
